02

Rene as a web designer & developer?

My inspiration didn't come as early as you may think — but the motivation came naturally. In Computer Engineering, Java and C++ were my first languages, and the coding process gave me a feeling of satisfaction, interest and enjoyment.

Then “System Analysis Design” taught me Agile, Waterfall, HTML5, CSS3, jQuery and PHP. Delivering a business website to a real client, as a team, was the first time I built something to a professional standard. Since then, I have aspired to become a web designer and developer.

Photoshop, layouts, logos — I've even practised drawing manga. Art & graphics have always been an interest of mine too.

Still young, always continuing my self-study to grow in this profession. To God be all the glory.
